The Night's Watch is an open community for the people who build and hold the data layer of web3: indexers, node and data-service operators, protocol and dapp devs, delegators, curators, and anyone serious about decentralised, verifiable, public data.

We're here to ship and to maintain: to help each other run things well, prototype new services, keep the lights on, and bring more people into the work.
